About Stagewrk

Stagewrk is a community-driven Roblox concert and event project focused on creating immersive virtual live-show experiences.

Our goal is to bring the atmosphere of real concerts into Roblox through custom-built stages, lighting, effects, virtual venues and interactive events.

Stagewrk is created by fans and developers who enjoy music, live events and building experiences within Roblox. We aim to create entertaining, high-quality experiences while respecting the artists, brands and communities that inspire our work.

Stagewrk is an independent project and is not operated by, sponsored by, or officially affiliated with any of the artists, bands, venues or companies represented in our experiences unless explicitly stated otherwise.
